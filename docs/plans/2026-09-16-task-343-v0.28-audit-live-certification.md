inactive removal removes one member and advances generation once; active,

order and currentness protocol. Target-bound lifecycle ownership is busy and
preserved; unrelated active-A state is not a global busy condition. The

remain intact. HostExplicit remains exactly 11. Frontend permission and UX
surfaces are command-specific, inactive-only, confirmation-gated, and
